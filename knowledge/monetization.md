# Monetization strategy

Evidence quality: most revenue figures online come from vendor marketing. This page records *structure and reasoning*; validate numbers with your own pilots.

## Recommendation
Build a **media brand** (audience asset) with a **product ladder**, add a **productized agency** service, and keep **coaching** as a late optional layer. Don't depend on platform payout programs.

| Path | Fit | Leverage | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital products (prompt packs, ComfyUI workflows, templates) | Fits the DM funnel exactly (keyword → DM → product) | High, near-zero marginal cost | needs a credible niche; license clearance for any shipped workflow |
| Productized agency ("done-for-you short-form / AI UGC ads") | The engine is the product; sells without a big following | High | client expectations; need brand-safe workflows and pilots |
| Brand deals / sponsorships | needs audience + engagement proof | Medium | AI-persona rates reportedly lower, audience skepticism of AI creators reportedly rising (vendor claims; validate) |
| Affiliate (tools; TikTok Shop if the niche fits) | Easy to start | Medium | disclosure rules; TikTok Shop follower thresholds vary |
| Merch / licensing of the persona | once the character has an audience | Medium | needs recognizable IP; POD fulfillment |
| Platform payouts (Creator Rewards, YouTube Partner, Meta bonuses) | upside only | Low and unreliable | templated/AI/unoriginal content is penalized or ineligible; eligibility unclear |
| 1:1 coaching | weakest fit | Low (time-bound) | credibility risk, doesn't use the engine |
| Community / cohort | after products exist | Medium | moderation load |

## Ladder
1. Free lead magnet via DM (pack/template) → email or community.
2. Tripwire product (low price).
3. Core product (workflow/template library).
4. Premium service (agency retainer, custom workflows).
5. Brand deals and affiliates layered on audience.
6. Merch/licensing when the character has pull.

## By niche (decided by `/niche-sprint`)
- **AI creators / tech:** products + agency + tool affiliates.
- **Entertainment / memes / pop culture:** reach-heavy; brand deals, merch, licensing; weakest direct-response.
- **Lifestyle / fashion / fitness / beauty:** TikTok Shop affiliate and brand deals; highest audience skepticism of AI personas; strictest claims rules.

## Validation plan
1. 30 days of baseline data.
2. 3 pilot offers via DM (free pack, $-tripwire, waitlist for a service).
3. 3 pilot agency clients at a discount in exchange for case studies.
4. Track revenue per 1k views and cost per approved video; keep what beats cost.

## Don't
Fabricate testimonials or results · promise income · hide that the persona is AI · rely on a single platform's program.
