# Facebook Reels, X, Threads

Launch these only after Instagram, TikTok and YouTube work. Everything here is **[TO CONFIRM]** against Blotato's current platform support and each platform's docs.

## Facebook Reels
Cross-posting from Instagram is typically simple through Meta tooling. Meta's originality rules reportedly apply across Facebook and Instagram (reduced reach and possible monetization limits for mostly unoriginal content). Use native phrasing, not byte-identical captions.

## X
Video posting limits (length, size, format) and API access tiers change often. Use short, text-forward captions. Check automation rules before scheduling; avoid reply or engagement automation.

## Threads
Text-first. A short caption plus video works; hashtags are lightly used. Check current API/Blotato support.

## Shared rules
- Per-platform caption variants; never identical text across accounts.
- Disclose the persona as AI in bios and where a platform offers an AI label.
- Don't automate follows, likes, or comments beyond the funnel's pinned/first comment.
