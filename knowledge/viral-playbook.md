# Viral playbook (craft, not guarantees)

Hypotheses to test, not laws. The `metrics-loop` skill decides what becomes a confirmed pattern for this persona.

## What the platforms reward (consistent across sources)
Retention (watch time, completion, re-watches), shares and sends, saves, comments, and follows per view. Original content is favored; reposts and mashups are down-ranked (`knowledge/compliance.md`).

## Hook types (taxonomy used in briefs)
pattern_interrupt · open_loop · bold_claim · relatable_pov · tutorial_promise · contrarian · curiosity_gap · transformation · social_proof. The hook lands in the first ~1–3 s, in both text and voice, and must work with sound off.

## Structure
Hook → tension/payoff in one idea → a reason to re-watch (loop back to the hook, a twist, a hidden detail). One idea per video. Cut dead air. Keep captions to ≤ 2 lines.

## Engagement triggers (pick one per video)
- **Share:** relatable or identity ("send this to the friend who…").
- **Comment:** a low-effort question, a mild disagreement bait that is safe, or the CTA keyword.
- **Save:** lists, steps, "come back to this".

## Length
Test buckets (≈7 s, ≈15 s, ≈30 s); the winner depends on the format and persona. Don't hard-code one.

## Trends and memes
- Lifecycle: emerging → rising → peak → declining. Enter early; skip peak and declining.
- Context matters: know a meme's origin and who it's about. When the origin is a tragedy, a real person's misfortune, politics or hate, skip it.
- Sounds are a major reach lever but come with rights and API limits (`platforms-*.md`). Default to original audio; add trending sounds as a human step on proven winners.
- A remix earns reach by adding something: the persona's angle, a niche application, a better payoff. A copy earns penalties.

## Persona consistency
Same face, voice, catchphrases, and recurring bits across videos build recognition. Variation comes from format and topic, not identity.

## Posting rhythm
Cadence is bounded by quality and by policy risk from templated output. Vary hooks, formats and lengths; avoid identical structure every day. Start with the lowest volume that lets the loop learn (a few videos per day across platforms), then scale on evidence.

## CTA design
One clear action: "Comment WORKFLOW". The keyword matches a funnel; the first comment repeats it. Offer something that is worth a DM (a pack, a template, a prompt list), not "follow for more".
